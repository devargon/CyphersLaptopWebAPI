Nothing stays hidden from Cypher. Not even your VALORANT Store.

Cypher's Laptop is a free Discord bot that keeps an eye on your VALORANT Store. Check it without launching the game, get a reminder when it resets, and spot the skins you've been waiting for.

Add to Discord

We never see your password.

What it keeps an eye on

VALORANT's Store gives you 4 random skins and changes every day. Cypher's Laptop brings it to Discord, so you don't have to launch the game to see if the skin you want is in.

/store

Check your VALORANT Store in Discord

See today's 4 skins with their rarity tier, price in VALORANT Points and a countdown to the next reset. Pick a currency in settings to see an approximate real-money price too.

/wishlist add

Wishlist the skins you want

Add skins by name and they're highlighted whenever they show up in your Store or Night Market.

/reminders

Know on reset day if your skin is in

Get a direct message every time your Store resets. Wishlisted skins are highlighted in it, so you know straight away whether today is the day.

/skin

Look up any weapon skin

Search every VALORANT weapon skin by name to see its rarity tier and price. Gun Buddies, Sprays, Player Cards and Player Titles are searchable with /accessory. No Riot login needed, so anyone in your server can use it.

Also on the laptop

Night Market
When the Night Market is on, /night-market shows your deals with their discounts. Skins start hidden and you reveal them one at a time, like in the game.
Wallet balance
/balance shows how many VALORANT Points and Radianite Points you have.
Prices in your currency
Choose a currency in /settings to see approximate real-money prices next to VP.
Hide your username
Show your Discord name instead of your Riot username when you check your Store in public channels.

How to get started

  1. Add Cypher's Laptop to a Discord server, or open a direct message with it.
  2. Run /login and sign in on Riot's own page. You never give the bot your password, and your region is detected for you. Unlink and delete your login data at any time with /logout.
  3. Run /store whenever you like, and turn on /reminders to get your Store when it resets.
